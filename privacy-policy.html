Privacy Policy – Al-Huda Quran

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Al-Huda Quran ("the App"), also known as الهدى للقرآن الكريم.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use its features.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and limiting the processing of information to what is necessary to provide the App's functionality.

2. Information We Access or Process

Depending on the features you use, Al-Huda Quran may access or process the following categories of information.

2.1 Precise Location

When you use features that require your location, such as prayer times and Qibla direction, the App may request access to your device's precise location.

The location is used to calculate prayer times and determine the Qibla direction.

According to the current implementation of the App, the location coordinates are processed locally on your device and are not sent to the App's backend server for these features.

The App does not request background location access for advertising.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time through your Android device settings. Location-dependent features may not function correctly without this permission.

2.2 Microphone and Audio Recordings

The App requests microphone access only when you choose to use the AI Recitation Evaluation feature.

The microphone is used to record your Quran recitation so that the requested evaluation can be performed.

When you submit a recording for evaluation, the audio is transmitted over an encrypted connection to the App's backend endpoint used for recitation evaluation. The backend then sends the audio for processing by the Google Gemini API in order to generate the requested evaluation.

The audio recording is processed for the requested evaluation and is not intended to be stored permanently in a database by the App.

Audio recordings are not intentionally used by the developer for advertising or unrelated purposes.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission through your Android device settings. Revoking the permission prevents use of features that require microphone access.

2.3 Locally Stored Information

The App stores certain information locally on your device to preserve your preferences and Quran-related progress. This may include:

  • Quran reading progress.
  • Last-read position.
  • Bookmarks and saved verses.
  • Application settings and preferences.
  • Selected reciter and translation preferences.
  • Reading streak and completed Juz progress.
  • Prayer and Qibla preferences.

The current implementation does not use Firebase, Firestore, Realtime Database, or other cloud databases to store this information.

Locally stored information remains on your device unless a feature explicitly sends information to an external service as described in this Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Android application uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) advertising technology to display advertisements.

Advertising services may process certain technical information and advertising identifiers associated with your device or interaction with advertisements, subject to Google's policies and applicable law.

Depending on your location, applicable law, your consent choices, and the configuration of the advertising service, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

The developer does not use your Quran reading progress, microphone recordings, or precise location for its own advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may communicate with third-party services to provide certain content and functionality.

These services currently include:

  • Quran.com API – used to retrieve Quran text, translations, tafsir, and related Quran content.
  • EveryAyah.com – used to provide Quran recitation audio files.
  • Google Gemini API – used for AI-powered features including recitation evaluation and other AI functionality.
  • Google advertising services – used to display advertisements where enabled.

Third-party services may process technical information necessary to provide their respective services and may be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

The App does not sell users' personal information.

5. Artificial Intelligence Features

Al-Huda Quran includes AI-powered features such as recitation evaluation.

When you voluntarily use an AI feature, information necessary to provide the requested functionality may be transmitted to the App's backend and processed through Google Gemini.

For recitation evaluation, this may include the audio recording submitted by you and technical information required to process the request.

AI processing is performed to provide the requested feature and is not intentionally used by the developer for advertising purposes.

Processing by Google may also be subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Communications between the App, its backend services, and supported external services are transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections where supported.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information stored locally by the App remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to the behavior of your Android device.

Audio submitted for AI recitation evaluation is processed for the requested evaluation and is not intended to be permanently stored in the App's database.

Third-party services may have their own retention policies for data processed through their services.

8. Data Deletion

Al-Huda Quran does not currently require users to create an account.

Because the App's user preferences and Quran progress are stored locally, you can delete this locally stored information by clearing the App's data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

There is currently no user account or cloud database maintained by the developer that requires an account deletion request.

If the App introduces account-based or cloud-storage functionality in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and an appropriate data-deletion mechanism will be provided where required.

9. Children's Privacy

Al-Huda Quran is a Quran and Islamic educational application intended for a general audience.

The App does not knowingly request children to provide personal information that is unnecessary for the App's functionality.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that is not permitted by applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to address and delete such information where required.

10. Your Choices and Permissions

You can control the App's access to device permissions through Android settings.

  • You may deny or revoke location permission.
  • You may deny or revoke microphone permission.
  • You may clear locally stored App data.
  • You may uninstall the App.
  • You may manage available advertising and privacy choices.

Some features may not work correctly if the required permission is denied or revoked.
